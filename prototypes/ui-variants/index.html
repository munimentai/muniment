Framed and Frameless

Two styles, each in light, dark, high contrast and vibrant tones. Preferences would offer the style and the tone as two choices. Each card shows the thread and Settings docked in the sidebar.

Live pages: press ⌘, or click Settings. Back to threads or Esc returns.
