milled ring · pulse + spin

The ring stays whole. Thinking is two independent random behaviors layered: breath — pulses that swell or slim (scale, stroke weight, and milling depth all flex both ways; a slim pulse smooths the teeth, a swell sharpens them) — and spin — rotation that drifts between fast, slow, slower, and still, occasionally reversing, easing between every change. Rare bright trace runs the outline once. Ink and still at rest; color and motion only while working.
